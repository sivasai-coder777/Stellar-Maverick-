NASA Challenge: Abandoned But Not Forgotten

Since the 1960s, NASA has left hardware across the solar system—on the Moon, on Mars, and in deep space. From rovers and instruments that completed their science missions to probes still traveling away from Earth, this hardware varies widely in both its purpose and current level of functionality. This interactive platform introduces school-age space enthusiasts to these incredible engineering feats, uncovering the structural anatomy of the hardware configurations and the revolutionary scientific space discoveries they made possible.
